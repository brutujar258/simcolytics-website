Simcolytics is a specialized energy consulting firm in Toronto, Canada with over 10 years of experience in battery energy storage systems (BESS) and photovoltaic (PV) design and implementation. Our team combines advanced research credentials in computational physics and machine learning with an MBA in Project & Strategic Management and professional certifications across project management and engineering.

The Name — Simcolytics

The name Simcolytics combines Simulation, Computation, and Analytics — the three disciplines at the core of our work. It is also a nod to Simcoe County, where the firm is rooted, while drawing on a decade of energy and analytics experience across India, the United States, and Canada.

We've managed high-risk engineering projects with budgets ranging from $500K to $2M USD, delivering comprehensive renewable energy solutions enhanced by predictive analytics. Our expertise spans the full lifecycle — feasibility, design, permitting, installation and commissioning — and our team has coordinated 150+ projects as subject-matter experts in complex energy storage design.

Our edge is the depth of our data science and AI/ML capability: cutting-edge ML, predictive analytics and intelligent energy management for energy optimization challenges, supported by cloud and AI certifications from leading technology providers.
